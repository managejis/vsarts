About This Service

Creative Arts by VS Arts

Since 1983, art has been at the heart of VS Arts. Our artists create customised wall art, canvas paintings, abstract works and pencil portraits that turn blank spaces into places with personality.

Every piece can be created to your theme, colours and size - modern or traditional, calm or bold. We work with homeowners, restaurants, clinics, offices and institutions, and can also design and install complete wall-art concepts for your space.

Why Choose Us

  • Original, handcrafted artwork
  • Customised themes, sizes and colours
  • Suitable for homes, offices, restaurants and institutions
  • Ready-to-hang or mounted and installed
  • Quality materials for lasting colour
  • Over four decades of artistic experience
