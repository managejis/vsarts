About This Service

Glow Signage by VS Arts

Your signboard is the first thing a customer sees. Our glow signage keeps your brand visible and attractive day and night, with bright, even LED lighting and a premium finish.

We design and make 2D flat signs, 3D raised letters and multi-layer 4D signage, as well as premium acrylic, aluminium and liquid-finish letters and neon wall features. Each sign is designed to suit your brand, made in our own workshop and installed by our team.

Why Choose Us

  • Bright, even and energy-efficient LED lighting
  • Weatherproof construction for outdoor use
  • Choice of 2D, 3D and 4D styles and many finishes
  • In-house design, fabrication and installation
  • Brand colours matched accurately
  • Day-and-night visibility that attracts customers
