About This Service

ACP Cladding by VS Arts

Aluminium Composite Panel (ACP) cladding gives a building a clean, modern and professional face. Two thin aluminium sheets bonded to a light core make the panels strong, flat, light in weight and resistant to sun, rain and dust, so the look stays sharp for years.

VS Arts designs, fabricates and installs ACP cladding for commercial buildings, showroom frontages, corporate offices and residential exteriors. We take care of the full job: site measurement, design, supporting frame, panel cutting and routing, installation and finishing, often together with your signage so the whole frontage looks like one design.

Why Choose Us

  • Lightweight, yet strong and perfectly flat
  • Weather, sun and moisture resistant
  • Wide range of colours, textures and metallic finishes
  • Low maintenance - easy to clean
  • Quick, tidy installation with minimal mess
  • Can be combined with glow signage for a complete frontage
