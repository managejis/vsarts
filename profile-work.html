About This Service

Profile Work by VS Arts

Profile work is all about precision. Using modern cutting equipment, we cut and shape aluminium, ACP, acrylic and decorative sheets into exact letters, logos, borders, jali patterns and architectural profiles - clean edges, perfect curves and consistent detail.

These profiles are used for signage letters, decorative wall features, partitions, nameplates, brand logos and building elements. We handle the cutting, the finishing and, if you wish, the installation.

Why Choose Us

  • Clean edges and accurate shapes every time
  • Wide choice of materials - aluminium, ACP, acrylic and more
  • Intricate designs and fine details possible
  • Finishes such as gold, silver, brushed and coloured
  • Works well with signage, cladding and interiors
  • Cut to size - from a single piece to bulk quantity
