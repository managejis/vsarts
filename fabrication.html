About This Service

Fabrication by VS Arts

Strong, accurate and well-finished metalwork is the backbone of every good installation. We specialise in custom fabrication in Mild Steel (MS), Stainless Steel (SS) and Aluminium - from a simple display frame to a complete aluminium partition cabin.

Every job is measured on site, cut and welded or assembled in our workshop with attention to alignment and finish, and then installed by our own team. We also fabricate the supporting structures for the signage and cladding we make, so everything fits together perfectly.

Why Choose Us

  • Work in MS, SS and aluminium under one roof
  • Accurate measurements and clean welds
  • Powder-coat, paint and polished finishes
  • Fabrication and installation by the same team
  • Made to your drawing or designed by us
  • Reliable delivery and after-sales support
